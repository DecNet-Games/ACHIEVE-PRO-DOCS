## 🔊 Opening the Sound & FX Studio

- **Top Menu**: `Tools > Achievement System Pro > Sound & FX Studio`

---

## 🎵 6 Procedural Waveform Sound Profiles

Audition synthesized sounds directly in Edit Mode:
1. 🔔 **Crystal Bell Chime**: Harmonic dual-tone bell with exponential decay ($880\text{Hz} + 1760\text{Hz}$).
2. 🚀 **Sci-Fi Warp Beep**: FM-modulated frequency sweep chirp.
3. 🕹️ **Retro 8-Bit Fanfare**: Fast 3-note chiptune arpeggio ($C_5 \to E_5 \to G_5$).
4. ⚡ **Sub-Bass Whoosh**: Low-frequency dynamic sweep for dramatic impacts.
5. 🏆 **Legendary Fanfare**: 4-part triumphant chord progression.
6. 💧 **Crisp Progress Tick**: 30ms micro-click for incremental progress bars.

---

## 💾 Exporting Directly to `.WAV`

1. Select your desired sound profile or customize frequency/pitch.
2. Click **"💾 Export to .WAV Asset"**.
3. The studio synthesizes PCM audio data and writes a standard uncompressed `.wav` file into `Assets/AchievementSystemPro/Audio/`, ready for assignment to themes or individual achievements!
