## 🚀 7 Built-In Factory Theme Presets

Generate production-ready themes with 1 click:
1. 🌆 **Cyberpunk Neon**: Obsidian slate, cyber yellow borders, neon cyan text, hot pink points.
2. 🌌 **Sci-Fi Deep Void**: Deep cosmos navy, laser blue glow, futuristic styling.
3. ⚔️ **Fantasy RPG Gold**: Dark walnut background, antique gold border, parchment typography.
4. 🎮 **Console Modern**: Minimalist PlayStation/Xbox inspired dark aesthetic.
5. ⚪ **Minimalist Frosted**: Clean monochrome frosted glass styling.
6. 🕹️ **Retro Arcade 8-Bit**: High-contrast pixel green / cyan palette.
7. 🥷 **Stealth Tactical**: Carbon fiber background with amber military telemetry.

## ▶️ In-Editor Animation Player

Test your UI animations **without entering Play Mode**:
- Click **"▶ Simulate Popup Animation"** to execute the real-time slide-in, spring bounce, display hold, and slide-out transitions directly inside the Editor window.
- Scrub the mock progress slider from `0%` to `100%` to preview progress bar filling.
